Pause

Privacy Policy — Last updated May 2026

Your health data stays on your device. Pause does not operate any servers or databases. We never see, store, or sell your personal information.

What Pause Collects

Pause collects only what you choose to enter directly:

  • Health & symptom data — daily logs, severity ratings, period tracking, mood, energy
  • Personal profile — first name, birth year, menopause stage, treatment information
  • Work profile — work pattern and role type, used to contextualise your daily guidance
  • Lifestyle milestones — treatments, diet changes, or other events you choose to log
  • Calendar events — meetings you add within the app for daily readiness guidance
  • AI API key — if you use AI Insights, your API key is stored locally on your device only

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Pause has no backend servers, no cloud database, and no account system.

Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the optional AI Insights feature.

Apple HealthKit

With your permission, Pause can read data from Apple Health — sleep, heart rate, and menstrual cycle data — to enrich your symptom patterns and daily readiness score.

Pause may also write your logged data back to Apple Health. HealthKit data is governed by Apple's privacy framework and is never used for advertising or shared outside the app.

AI Insights (Optional)

If you choose to enable AI-powered insights, Pause sends a contextual summary of your symptom patterns to a third-party AI provider using the API key you supply. The available providers and their privacy policies are:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • OpenAI
  • Google (Gemini)

This feature is entirely optional. Pause is fully functional without it.

Notifications

Pause uses local notifications only. Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device based on your logged data. No notification data is sent to any external server.

What Pause Does Not Do

  • Collect data on our servers
  • Sell your data
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use tracking or analytics SDKs
  • Require an account or login

Children

Pause is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
