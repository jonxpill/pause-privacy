Common Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to turn on AI Insights. There's no account and no login.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Reset all data" to permanently erase your logs, profile and strategies from your device, including what Siri and the widget show. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Do I need to pay?

Pause is free for 30 days with full access, including built-in AI. After the trial, a one-time purchase keeps the app. Built-in AI is an optional monthly subscription — or you can use your own AI key, Apple Intelligence, or no AI at all.

What are the AI options?

Built-in AI (sent through Pause's relay to Anthropic's Claude), Apple Intelligence (on your device), your own API key, or none. Pause asks before sending anything to an AI company, and works fully without AI.

How do I add the widget?

Open Pause once after updating. Then touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Pause. The larger size has one-tap log buttons; touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget to pick which two symptoms it shows.

How do I use Siri with Pause?

Try “Log brain fog in Pause”, “How am I doing in Pause” or “What were my most common symptoms last week in Pause”. To let Siri and Spotlight understand your logged days, turn on Settings → Siri & Apple Intelligence inside Pause (iOS 18 or later). Your notes themselves are never included.

Can I check a report before I send it?

Yes. In the You tab, tap “Generate symptom report”, pick your options and tap Generate. The report opens in Pause first; tap Share only when you're ready to email or save it.

Does Pause give medical advice?

No. Pause is a personal tracking and insight tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always speak to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

How do I restore a purchase?

On a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore Purchases" (also available on the unlock screen).
